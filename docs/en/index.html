ZinggumDol

Missing a step doesn't mean you fell in.
A gentle journal for the days that didn't go to plan, where you talk things through with a little pebble.

Download on the App Store

What it does

Talk it over with a pebble

The pebble speaks first. It remembers what you told it last time and asks how things went since. Pick a reply or write your own.

Just say it

On days when writing feels like too much, say it out loud. Speech is recognized on your device, and on supported devices your words are tidied up so they read easily.

The way you've come

Each week is laid out as a row of stepping stones. Days you didn't step on stay there too; nothing gets wiped away.

What you've left

Look back at what happened, how you felt and what you'll try next, all in date order.

Widget and reminders

See a word from the pebble on your Home Screen, and get a gentle nudge at a time you choose to look at how today went.

Yours alone

No sign-up. Your records live only on your device and in your own iCloud, and you can lock the app with a passcode.
